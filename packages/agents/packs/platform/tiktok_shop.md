# TikTok Shop platform pack

## How selling works here
- In-app marketplace inside TikTok. Discovery-driven: the For You feed pushes products through short videos and LIVE; Shop tab search is secondary but growing.
- Content is the storefront: own videos, affiliate videos, LIVE, Shop tab search, ads.
- TikTok owns the customer: checkout, messaging, and buyer data stay on-platform; contact details are masked.
- What wins: visually demonstrable products, impulse prices (~$10–50), a 1–3 s hook, many creators posting at once, fast shipping. Content volume beats polish.

## Fees and payouts
- Referral fee ~6–8% of order value in most US categories (includes payment processing); new sellers sometimes get reduced intro rates.
- Affiliate commission is seller-set: ~10–20% open, ~15–30% targeted, plus sample cost.
- Fulfilled by TikTok (FBT) charges per-unit fulfillment and storage.
- Discounts, vouchers, and ad spend come out of margin.
- Plan total take (fee + commission + discounts + ads) at ~25–45% of price.
- Payouts settle after delivery plus a hold (often ~1–2 weeks; longer for new or low-score shops); reserves possible.
- Fees change — check the current schedule before quoting exact numbers.

## Listing rules
- Title up to ~255 chars; mobile shows ~40–60. Core keyword + key attribute + use/benefit. No ALL CAPS, emojis, promo words ("hot", "best", "free"), or other brands.
- Images: up to ~9, square, ≥~600 px (1200+ ideal); main image on clean background, no watermarks, borders, or text collages. Add a 9:16 product video.
- Description: benefits, specs, box contents; size chart required for apparel. No external links, phone numbers, or off-platform payment.
- Fill all category attributes; wrong category means delisting or reduced reach.
- Variants only for real size/color/pack options, each with own SKU, price, stock, image.
- Selling a brand you don't own needs authorization; IP complaints remove listings and add violation points.
- Restricted (approval needed): beauty/skincare, supplements, food, jewelry, battery electronics. Prohibited: weapons, drugs/CBD, tobacco/vapes, adult, live animals, counterfeits.
- Claims: no medical/curative claims, exaggerated before/after, unproven "No.1"/"best", or fake strikethrough prices (reference price must match a recent real selling price, ~30 days).

## Policies and account health
- Ship within the set handling time (commonly 1–3 business days) with valid tracking.
- Targets (approx.): late dispatch <~4%, seller-fault cancellation <~2.5%, valid tracking >~95%, buyer replies within ~12–24 h.
- Shop Performance Score (1–5) blends review ratings, fulfillment, and service; low scores lose campaign, affiliate, and ad eligibility.
- Account Health Rating accrues violation points (listings, content, fulfillment, IP); thresholds bring warnings, feature restrictions, then permanent deactivation.
- Returns: buyers typically have ~15–30 days; unanswered requests auto-approve after ~48 h; low-value items may be refund-only.
- Creators must disclose paid partnerships; their guideline violations hit the shop.
- Never divert buyers off-platform (external links, QR codes, codes for other sites in packages).

## Ads and growth levers
- GMV Max: automated Shop campaigns optimizing gross revenue to a target ROI; the default Shop ad type. Uses seller and authorized creator videos. LIVE GMV Max for streams.
- Spark Ads: boost creator posts via authorization codes.
- Affiliates: open collaboration (any eligible creator), targeted collaboration (invited, higher rate), free-sample requests; Creator Marketplace for discovery.
- LIVE selling: scheduled ~1–3 h streams with pinned products and flash deals.
- Promotions: product discounts, Flash Deals, shop/product vouchers, bundle deals, free-shipping thresholds. Platform campaigns (Deals for You Days, Black Friday/Cyber Monday) require price commitments.
- Reviews: in-app requests, feature review videos; never incentivize.

## Playbook notes by role
- Creative Studio: 9:16, native creator style, hook in ~1–3 s, product in hand by ~3 s, captions on; ship 5–10 variants per hero product weekly. Fatigue is fast.
- Ad Strategist: set GMV Max ROI above break-even after fees and commission; feed it the best organic and affiliate videos instead of ad-only creative.
- Product Hunter: pick demonstrable "wow" products with ~4–5x markup over landed cost; the fee/commission stack eats ~25–45%.
- Copywriter: keyword in title and first description line; no health claims or superlatives; keep spoken scripts compliant too.
- Operations: hold ~2–4 weeks of stock before pushing affiliates; viral spikes cause late dispatch and cancellations. Prefer FBT or 1-day handling for hero SKUs.
- Market Analyst: split GMV by source (own video, affiliate, LIVE, product card/search, ads); track GPM and per-creator GMV.
- Email & SMS: no buyer contact data; retarget with in-app messages and vouchers. Build owned lists only from separate DTC traffic.
