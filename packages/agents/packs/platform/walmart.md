# Walmart platform pack

## How selling works here
- Search-driven marketplace on Walmart.com and app; application required (business verification, marketplace track record).
- Many sellers share one item page; the Buy Box goes to the best mix of landed price, delivery speed, and seller performance.
- Walmart owns the customer: no marketing contact, no external links.
- Price is king: Walmart compares against Walmart.com and other retailers and suppresses offers it judges uncompetitive.
- What wins: competitive landed price, 2-day delivery tags (WFS or fast self-ship), complete content (high Listing Quality score), reviews, steady stock. Less competition than Amazon, lower traffic.

## Fees and payouts
- No monthly, setup, or listing fee.
- Referral fee ~6–15% by category, most ~15%; lower tiers for cheap apparel.
- Walmart Fulfillment Services (WFS): ~$3.50+ per unit by weight; storage ~$0.75 per cu ft monthly, ~$2.25 Oct–Dec; long-term storage surcharges.
- Ads (Walmart Connect) and promotions come out of margin.
- Plan total take (referral + WFS + ads) at ~25–40% of price.
- Payouts roughly every 14 days for delivered orders; holds possible on new or flagged accounts.
- Fees change — check the current schedule before quoting exact numbers.

## Listing rules
- Product name ~50–90 chars: Brand + product type + defining attribute + size/count. No promo words, ALL CAPS, or keyword stuffing.
- Key Features: 3–10 short bullets (aim 5+), benefit-led.
- Description: ~150+ words, scannable.
- Images: ≥4 (more is better), square, ~2,200 px recommended for zoom; main image on white background, no text or watermarks. Video and 360 views raise the score.
- Attributes: complete every category-specific attribute; they drive filters and Listing Quality.
- GTIN (GS1-verified UPC) required; mismatches get unpublished.
- Variants: group under one Variant Group ID with valid variant attributes (color, size, count).
- Never mention other marketplaces, external URLs, or competitor brands.
- Rich content (enhanced/A+-style) via Walmart Brand Portal for brand owners.
- Prohibited/restricted: firearms and ammo, alcohol, tobacco/vapes, CBD, recalled or unsafe goods, counterfeits; some categories need approval.
- Pricing rules: items priced well above market or reference price get unpublished; fix price, then republish.

## Policies and account health
- Seller Performance Standards (approx.): on-time delivery ≥95%, valid tracking ≥99%, cancellations ≤2%, ≥95% of messages answered within 48 h, refund rate ≤~6%.
- Misses bring warnings, a Performance Improvement Plan, then suspension or termination.
- Set fulfillment lag (1–2 days typical) and ship by the ship-by date; tracking must scan.
- Returns: follow Walmart's ~30-day policy floor; respond fast or Walmart refunds at seller cost. WFS handles returns and service.
- Pro Seller badge (monthly): strong delivery, tracking, cancellations, content quality, competitive price, fast-ship assortment, ratings.
- Reviews: no incentives; use Spark Reviewer where eligible.

## Ads and growth levers
- Walmart Connect Sponsored Products: CPC auction, automatic and manual (keyword) campaigns; auto first, harvest terms into manual.
- Sponsored Brands (Search Brand Amplifier) and Sponsored Videos for registered brands; Display via DSP.
- Fast-shipping tags: WFS or 2-day self-fulfillment lifts conversion and rank.
- Price levers: built-in Repricer (rules vs competitors), Reduced Price/Rollback displays, Walmart Deals events (Walmart+ Week, holiday).
- Listing Quality score as the organic dashboard: content, offer, ratings, post-purchase.
- Spark Reviewer seeds reviews on new items.

## Playbook notes by role
- Copywriter: shorter titles than Amazon (~50–90 chars); adapt Amazon copy: cut keyword stuffing, keep 5+ crisp features, remove any Amazon reference.
- Ad Strategist: start auto campaigns at modest bids, move converting terms to manual exact, cut non-converting; price and 2-day tag matter before ads.
- Operations: hit valid tracking ~99% and on-time ≥95%; use WFS for top SKUs to win 2-day tags; keep stock synced to avoid cancellations.
- Market Analyst: monitor Listing Quality score, Buy Box ownership, price competitiveness vs other retailers weekly.
- Product Hunter: favor everyday-value goods with GTINs and margin at Walmart-competitive prices; avoid categories needing approval unless ready.
- Creative Studio: white-background main, 4+ secondaries (lifestyle, scale, infographic), short video; reuse Amazon assets where compliant.
- Email & SMS: no contact with Walmart buyers; grow owned lists from DTC channels only.
