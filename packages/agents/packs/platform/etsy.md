# Etsy platform pack

## How selling works here
- Marketplace for handmade, vintage (20+ years), craft supplies, and designed/print-on-demand items. Buyers arrive and search; most traffic is Etsy search and app.
- Etsy owns the customer: no marketing to buyers without opt-in; messaging stays on Etsy.
- Ranking: query match (title, tags, attributes, category), listing quality (CTR, favorites, sales), customer and market experience (reviews, cases, complete shop and policies), shipping price, recency, personalization.
- What wins: gifts, personalization, niche aesthetics, giftable prices (~$20–80), standout thumbnails, free US shipping on ~$35+ orders, Star Seller reliability.

## Fees and payouts
- Listing fee ~$0.20, renews every 4 months or per unit sold on multi-quantity listings.
- Transaction fee ~6.5% of item + shipping + gift wrap.
- Payment processing ~3% + $0.25 (US; varies by country).
- Offsite Ads ~15% of order (~12% and mandatory above ~$10k sales in 12 months), capped ~$100 per order; charged when a sale follows an ad click within ~30 days.
- Etsy Ads CPC, budget from ~$1/day.
- Also: currency conversion ~2.5%, regulatory and setup fees in some countries. Share & Save credits back ~4% on shared-link sales.
- Plan total take ~10–13%, ~25%+ on Offsite Ads orders.
- Payouts daily to monthly; new or high-case shops can face reserves.
- Fees change — check the current schedule before quoting exact numbers.

## Listing rules
- Title max 140 chars; Etsy favors short, readable titles (~under 15 words). Lead with what the item is, then the key descriptor. No repeated keywords or ALL CAPS.
- Tags: 13, ≤20 chars each, multi-word long-tail phrases. Don't duplicate attributes or category (they already count).
- Attributes: fill every relevant one (color, occasion, holiday, material, style); free search terms that power filters.
- Photos: up to ~20 plus one 5–15 s silent video; ≥2000 px shortest side. First photo is the thumbnail; center the subject so square and 4:3 crops both work.
- Description: first ~160 chars feed Google snippets; state item, size, materials, personalization steps, processing time.
- Variations: up to 2 types with per-option price, quantity, SKU; personalization box for custom text.
- Creativity Standards: each item must be "made by", "designed by" (POD included; disclose production partners), "sourced by", or "handpicked by" (vintage/supplies). Mass-produced resale gets listings removed or shops suspended.
- Prohibited: IP infringement (characters, sports teams, brand logos, celebrity names), medical claims, weapons, drugs, hate content, recalled items.

## Policies and account health
- Set accurate processing time (~1–5 days typical); ship with tracking by the dispatch date. Etsy labels auto-upload tracking.
- Star Seller (monthly, prior 3 months, needs ~5+ orders and ~$300 sales): ≥95% first replies within 24 h, ≥95% on-time shipments with tracking, ≥4.8 average rating.
- Purchase Protection refunds buyers for undelivered or not-as-described orders; high case rates or poor ratings bring reserves, search demotion, or suspension.
- Publish returns/exchanges policy (window ~14–30 days); personalized items can be non-returnable.
- Reviews: open ~100 days after delivery; never incentivize; one calm public reply.
- Messages: order-related only; no unsolicited marketing; never steer buyers to purchase off-Etsy (fee avoidance).

## Ads and growth levers
- Etsy Ads: onsite promoted listings, CPC with Etsy-managed bids. Start ~$1–5/day on proven converters; pause listings that spend without sales.
- Offsite Ads: Google, Meta, Pinterest, Bing; opt-out only under ~$10k annual sales. Price the fee in.
- Sales and discounts: % off sales (strikethrough), coupon codes, automated targeted offers (abandoned cart, favorited, thank-you).
- Free Shipping Guarantee (US orders ~$35+) improves US placement.
- Seasonality: list holiday items ~6–8 weeks early (Q4, Valentine's, Mother's Day); rotate seasonal tags.

## Playbook notes by role
- Copywriter: short clear title, 13 non-redundant long-tail tags, every attribute filled. Buyer language ("gift for her", "personalized") beats jargon.
- Creative Studio: thumbnail decides CTR. Bright styled lifestyle shot, product filling the frame, consistent shop look; show scale and personalization options.
- Ad Strategist: price in the ~15% Offsite Ads fee; judge Etsy Ads per listing after ~2–4 weeks; keep spend on proven converters.
- Product Hunter: favor personalization, gift occasions, niche designs. POD must clear Creativity Standards and IP checks.
- Operations: protect Star Seller: reply under 24 h, ship on time with tracking, pad processing time in Q4.
- Email & SMS: buyers aren't the store's list; use Etsy targeted offers. Collect opt-ins only on an owned site.
- Market Analyst: track Etsy Stats per listing: views → favorites → orders; conversion ~1–3% typical.
