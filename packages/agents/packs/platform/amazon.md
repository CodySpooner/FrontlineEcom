# Amazon platform pack

## How selling works here
- Largest search-driven marketplace. Buyers arrive with intent; rank rewards sales velocity, conversion rate, relevance, price, and in-stock Prime availability.
- Fulfillment: FBA (Prime badge; Amazon ships, handles returns and service) or FBM / Seller Fulfilled Prime.
- Sellers share product pages (ASINs); the Buy Box goes to the best mix of price, delivery speed, and account health.
- Amazon owns the customer: no buyer emails, no marketing contact.
- What wins: high-converting listing (main image, title, reviews, price), Brand Registry, no stockouts, disciplined PPC, early reviews.

## Fees and payouts
- Plan: Professional ~$39.99/mo, or Individual ~$0.99 per item sold.
- Referral fee ~15% in most categories (range ~8–45%; lower tiers for cheap apparel), minimum ~$0.30.
- FBA fulfillment ~$3–7+ per standard-size unit by size/weight; oversize far more.
- Storage ~$0.75–1 per cu ft monthly Jan–Sep, ~$2.40 Oct–Dec; aged-inventory surcharge after ~180 days; low-inventory fee under ~4 weeks of supply; inbound placement fees.
- Also: returns processing (high-return categories), coupon, deal, and Vine fees.
- Plan total take (referral + FBA + storage + ads) at ~35–50% of price.
- Payouts every ~14 days (daily on request); funds release ~7 days after delivery.
- Fees change — check the current schedule before quoting exact numbers.

## Listing rules
- Title ≤~200 chars (some categories shorter): Brand + product type + key feature + size/count/color. No promo phrases, ALL CAPS, special characters (! $ ? etc.), or any word more than twice.
- Bullets: 5, benefit-first, ~150–250 chars each; no pricing, shipping, or guarantee promises.
- Description ~2,000 chars; A+ Content (Brand Registry) replaces it with modules, comparison charts, Brand Story.
- Backend search terms <250 bytes: synonyms, misspellings, use cases. No brand names, ASINs, competitors, or repeats.
- Images: main on pure white (RGB 255), product ~85% of frame, no text, logos, or props; ≥1000 px for zoom (~2000 ideal); up to ~9 images plus video.
- GTIN (GS1 UPC/EAN) required unless exempt via Brand Registry.
- Variations: parent/child only for real themes (size, color, count). Merging unrelated products to pool reviews is abuse.
- Gated or restricted: grocery, topical beauty, supplements (third-party testing), jewelry, watches, toys in Q4, hazmat/batteries (SDS), pesticide claims ("antibacterial"), medical devices. Prohibited: drug or "FDA approved" claims, counterfeits, recalled goods.

## Policies and account health
- Order Defect Rate <1% (negative feedback + A-to-z claims + chargebacks): the main suspension trigger.
- FBM: late shipment <4%, pre-fulfillment cancel <2.5%, valid tracking >95%, on-time delivery >~90%.
- Answer buyer messages within 24 h, weekends included.
- Account Health Rating 0–1000: keep 200+; under ~100 risks deactivation. IP, authenticity, and safety complaints count.
- Returns: ~30-day minimum window (extended for holidays); many returns auto-authorize.
- Reviews: no incentives, swaps, or filtering for happy buyers; no inserts asking for 5 stars. Use "Request a Review" or one compliant message; Vine for new ASINs.
- Buyer-Seller Messaging is order-related only: no marketing, no external links.
- FBA: respect capacity limits; prep and label to spec.

## Ads and growth levers
- Sponsored Products: CPC; auto plus manual keyword/product targeting; exact/phrase/broad; placement bid adjustments.
- Sponsored Brands (headline, video, Store spotlight) and Sponsored Display (retargeting, audiences) mostly need Brand Registry; DSP for scale.
- Promotions: coupons, Prime Exclusive Discounts, Lightning/Best Deals, Prime Day and holiday events; Subscribe & Save for consumables.
- Brand Registry unlocks A+, Stores, Brand Analytics, Vine, Sponsored Brands, Brand Tailored Promotions.
- External traffic: Amazon Attribution and Brand Referral Bonus (~10% credit on attributed sales).

## Playbook notes by role
- Ad Strategist: launch SP auto + exact on core terms; harvest converting search terms weekly into exact, add negatives. Keep ACoS ≤ pre-ad margin; manage to TACoS (~8–15% when mature).
- Copywriter: main keyword in the first ~80 title chars (mobile cut-off); fill backend terms to near 250 bytes.
- Creative Strategist: compliant main image; secondaries = infographic, lifestyle, scale, comparison, box contents. A+ banners ~970x600.
- Product Researcher: run FBA fee math before sourcing; avoid oversize, gated, hazmat, and niches where top listings hold thousands of reviews.
- Operations: hold ~60–90 days of cover including inbound lead time; stockouts tank rank and Buy Box. Watch ODR daily.
- Operations: orders with supplier "Amazon FBA" are shipped by Amazon. A late or lost one is a Seller Central case for the owner, not a supplier to chase, so keep FBA out of the supplier scorecard and backup suppliers. Store sales come from store_summary, not from summing order totals.
- Analytics Specialist: use Search Query Performance for share of search; track BSR, sessions, unit session %, Buy Box %.
- Email & SMS: no marketing to Amazon buyers; grow owned lists from DTC traffic; re-engage via Brand Tailored Promotions and Store followers.
