# Fitness niche pack

## Customer
- Home-workout beginners (25-45, female-skewed, largest DTC segment): low intimidation, small space, short routines; bands, mats, walking pads, wall pilates.
- Lifters and home-gym builders (20-45, male-skewed): spec- and brand-savvy, gimmick-skeptical; adjustable dumbbells, racks, grips.
- Women 35-60: strength for longevity, weighted vests, pilates, walking; trust credentialed coaches.
- Recovery and pain relief: massage guns, foam rollers, compression, mobility; runners and older adults.
- Hangouts: TikTok (GymTok, PilatesTok), Instagram, YouTube, Reddit (r/homegym, r/fitness, r/running), Strava.
- Motivation: identity and transformation, convenience (no gym), confidence, pain relief.

## Buying triggers and objections
- Triggers: New Year and other fresh starts, a creator's easy routine, space-saving demo, a trend (rucking, wall pilates), an injury.
- "I'll stop using it" -> included program (PDF or QR videos), 30-day challenge, community.
- "Sturdy and safe?" -> weight capacity, materials, load test, warranty.
- "No space" -> fold/store demo, dimensions in inches.
- "Gimmick?" -> explain the mechanism, honest expectations, coach demo.
- "Heavy or pricey" -> free shipping, clear delivery date, installments above ~$150.

## Compliance and claims
- FTC weight-loss "gut check" claims are never acceptable: loss without diet or exercise, permanent loss, blocking fat/calories, loss for everyone, over 3 lbs/week for over 4 weeks, loss from wearing or rubbing on a product (sauna suits, waist trainers, slimming creams). Any weight or body-composition claim needs scientific substantiation.
- Testimonials must reflect generally expected results; "results not typical" doesn't fix an atypical one.
- Supplements (DSHEA): structure/function claims only ("supports muscle recovery") with the FDA disclaimer and the brand's 30-day FDA notification; no disease claims; Supplement Facts panel; cGMP manufacturing (21 CFR 111). Seek NSF Certified for Sport or Informed Sport.
- Devices: EMS ab belts, red-light panels, compression boots may be FDA-regulated; "burns fat", "six-pack", "inch loss", "treats pain" need clearance. Wellness wearables may track steps and sleep; never claim blood glucose, blood pressure or AFib detection without clearance (FDA warned about non-invasive glucose watches).
- Equipment: product liability is the main risk. Publish weight limits, assembly steps and warnings; request load tests and ISO 20957/ASTM reports (verify which apply); carry product liability insurance; check CPSC recalls.
- Ads: Meta and TikTok restrict weight-loss content (18+, no before/after, no "lose X lbs in Y days", no personal-attribute callouts like "Tired of your belly fat?"). TikTok bans weight-loss supplement promotion.
- EU/UK: CE/UKCA for powered gear; supplement health claims limited to an approved EU/UK list.

## Seasonality
- Late Dec-Jan: biggest window. Creatives built in November, stock landed early December, scale Dec 26-Jan 31 (CPMs drop after Q4).
- Feb: drop-off after mid-January; retarget with "restart" angles.
- Mar-May: "summer body", spring races.
- Jun-Aug: home-gym dip; portable gear up; Prime Day (July).
- Sep: "September reset" second peak, fall races.
- Nov-Dec: BFCM for big-ticket equipment; gifts (massage guns, wearables, vests).

## Creative angles
- 15-30s follow-along workout using the product.
- Space demo: unfold, use, slide under the bed.
- Coach or physical therapist explains form and why it works.
- Progress in strength, energy and consistency, not body transformations.
- Examples: "10-minute walking pad routine while I answer emails"; "My whole home gym fits in this closet"; "Physical therapist ranks massage guns"; "30 days with a weighted vest: my walking pace"; "Wall pilates for absolute beginners, day 1".

## Price norms and margins
- Accessories (bands, grips, ropes, mats) $15-$45; mid (massage guns, vests, kettlebells) $50-$150; equipment (walking pads, adjustable dumbbells, benches, rowers) $150-$700.
- AOV $55-$90 for accessory stores, $200+ for equipment stores.
- Landed COGS 25-40%; on heavy items shipping can be 15-30% of price; quote dimensional weight before listing.
- Bundles: starter kit (bands + mat + sliders), second-item discount, digital program upsell (near-100% margin).
- Compare-at only for real prior prices.

## Products and suppliers
- Evergreen: resistance bands, mats, dumbbells/kettlebells, foam rollers, jump ropes, grips, gym bags.
- Trend: walking pads, weighted vests, wall pilates bars, mini steppers, foldable reformers, rucking plates; 3-12 month curves, test small.
- Pitfalls: bands snapping (latex quality), rubber smell, peeling coatings, weld/pin failures, inaccurate weights, walking pad motor/belt failures.
- Return drivers: heavier/bulkier than expected, assembly frustration, defects, "not sturdy". Return freight on heavy items can exceed margin; ask for photos, offer parts or partial refunds.
- Shipping: heavy goods from a US warehouse; China-direct only for light accessories.
- Vet: load and fatigue test reports, weight tolerance (about +/-3%), warranty and spare parts, samples tested by a real lifter.

## Red flags
- Weight-loss guarantees, sauna suits or waist trainers sold for fat loss, slimming creams/patches, EMS "abs without exercise".
- SARMs, prohormones, DMAA/DMHA stimulants, "GLP-1" supplements or patches.
- Transformation before/after ads, body-shaming copy.
- Unlisted electrical equipment, products without weight limits, unapproved medical claims on wearables.
