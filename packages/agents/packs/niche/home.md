# Home niche pack

## Customer
- Segments: "home refresh" women 30–55 (largest group), first apartment/new homeowners 25–40, renters wanting no-drill upgrades, organizing/cleaning enthusiasts, gift buyers (housewarming, wedding, holidays).
- Care about: how it looks in their room, size and scale, material feel, easy install and cleaning, not looking cheap.
- Hang out: Pinterest (planning, high intent), TikTok/Instagram (#homedecor, CleanTok, apartment tours), Reddit (r/InteriorDesign, r/BuyItForLife), Facebook groups (40+).
- Motivation: the Pinterest look on a budget, fixing clutter/lighting/storage, seasonal refresh, moving.

## Buying triggers and objections
- Triggers: before/after transformation, "renter-friendly", viral aesthetic item, restock, free-shipping threshold.
- "Will it fit or look right?" → dimensions (in and cm) on an image, scale shots, styled room photos.
- "Looks cheap in person" → texture close-up video, material specs (solid wood vs MDF, fabric gsm), customer photos.
- "Arrives broken" → drop-tested packaging, free-replacement promise, photo-claim flow.
- "Slow shipping" → US stock or honest ETA; 2–3 week waits kill decor impulse buys.
- "Cheaper at IKEA/Target/Amazon" → unique design, bundle value, warranty.

## Compliance and claims
- Electrical (lamps, diffusers, heaters, smart plugs): sell UL/ETL (NRTL) listed products. Not always legally required, but marketplaces and insurers expect it; verify the listing before claiming it. Radios/Bluetooth/LED controllers need FCC Part 15.
- Prop 65: show the California warning before purchase when a listed chemical may exceed safe harbor (lead in brass or glaze, DEHP in PVC, formaldehyde in pressed wood).
- Furniture: clothing storage units 27 in+ must meet the STURDY Act tip-over rule (16 CFR 1261): stability testing, warning label, anchor kit. Composite wood: TSCA Title VI formaldehyde label. Upholstered furniture flammability: 16 CFR 1640; mattresses: 16 CFR 1632/1633.
- Candles: lead-core wicks banned; follow ASTM F2058 labeling and F2417 fire safety (voluntary, expected).
- Food contact: FDA-compliant materials; "lead-free"/"food safe" only with test reports. State PFAS cookware bans/disclosure rules (MN, CO, CA, others); verify.
- Germ/virus-killing claims (UV sanitizers, "antibacterial", purifiers) make it an EPA FIFRA pesticide device. California requires CARB certification for air cleaners. No allergy/asthma/mold health claims.
- Window blinds: sell cordless (strangulation risk; verify current CPSC rules).
- EU/UK: CE/UKCA, GPSR responsible person, WEEE for electricals, UK plugs.

## Seasonality
- Jan: "organize the new year" peak (storage, cleaning).
- Feb–Mar: spring refresh starts; Easter decor.
- Apr–May: spring cleaning, patio/outdoor living, Mother's Day (candles, throws, kitchen).
- Jun–Aug: moving season peak, patio/garden, Prime Day (July) price pressure, dorm/back-to-college (late Jul–Aug).
- Sep–Oct: fall/cozy decor (Pinterest from mid-Aug), Halloween, Prime Big Deal Days (Oct).
- Nov–Dec: BFCM and holiday decor/gifting, often 30–40% of annual revenue. Christmas decor sells Oct to mid-Dec, then dies.
- Prep: Pinterest content 45–90 days ahead; Q4 stock ordered Aug–Sep; order by early Dec to cover Chinese New Year shutdown (late Jan–Feb).

## Creative angles
- Formats: before/after makeovers, ASMR (cleaning, organizing, unboxing), room-tour UGC, styled Pinterest pins.
- Proof: customer room photos, dimension overlays, material close-ups, review counts.
- Angles:
  1. "My landlord said no paint, so I did this": no-drill transformation.
  2. "The $40 version of the $400 look": dupe the look, never the brand name.
  3. "POV: you finally fixed the junk drawer": organizing ASMR.
  4. "One mirror, three rooms": boho vs modern vs minimal.
  5. "Lights off" reveal: lamp turns a room cozy.

## Price norms and margins
- Bands: small decor/gadgets $15–40; textiles $25–60; lighting $40–120; wall art $30–150; rugs/furniture $100–500+.
- AOV: $55–90; free-shipping threshold ~20–30% above median item price.
- Landed COGS: 20–35% of price, bulky/fragile up to 40%; price at 2.5–4x landed. US de minimis ended Aug 2025, so include duty and current tariffs (verify) in landed cost.
- Compare-at: 20–40% above, only if a real former/market price (FTC 16 CFR 233, CA law).
- Bundles: sets of 2/4 (pillow covers, bins), "complete the look" kits, add-ons (bulbs, hooks, candles); AOV lift ~15–30%.

## Products and suppliers
- Evergreen: storage/organization, lighting (rechargeable, LED), throws/bedding, kitchen gadgets, wall decor, cleaning tools.
- Trend: viral aesthetic pieces (mushroom lamps, wavy mirrors), seasonal decor; 2–6 month life.
- Quality pitfalls: color mismatch, thin fabric, flaking plating, wobbly assembly, foam/plastic odor, wrong plug or voltage.
- Return drivers: "looks different than pictured", size, transit damage. Bulky returns rarely pay to ship back; set a refund-and-keep threshold.
- Shipping: dimensional weight kills margin on big, light items (vacuum-pack textiles); double-box glass and ceramics.
- Vetting: order samples, drop-test packaging, collect test reports (UL/ETL file no., Prop 65, TSCA VI, FDA/LFGB), prefer US warehouse stock.

## Red flags
- Unlisted space heaters, extension cords, power strips, cheap lithium gadgets.
- Germ-killing, air-purifying, allergy or "non-toxic" claims without proof.
- Tall dressers without anti-tip compliance; infant sleep items sold as decor.
- Designer names or trademarks in titles; AI renders as product photos.
