# Fashion niche pack

## Customer
- Segments: women 18–34 trend shoppers (TikTok/IG, price-sensitive); women 35–55 (fit, comfort, flattering, higher AOV, Facebook); men's basics/streetwear (fit, durability, fewer returns); plus-size (underserved, loyal once fit works); occasion buyers (wedding guest, vacation, festival).
- Care about: fit, how it looks on a body like theirs, fabric feel, true color, easy exchanges, arriving before the event.
- Hang out: TikTok (GRWM, try-on hauls), Instagram Reels, Pinterest outfit boards, Reddit (r/femalefashionadvice, r/malefashionadvice).
- Motivation: self-expression, an upcoming event, seasonal refresh, trend FOMO, comfort upgrade.

## Buying triggers and objections
- Triggers: seeing it on a relatable body, event deadline, new drop or restock, viral item, free shipping plus free exchanges, BNPL.
- "Will it fit?" → per-garment size chart (in and cm), model height and size worn, fit notes ("runs small, size up"), reviews with height/weight.
- "Fabric looks cheap" → close-up and stretch videos, fiber % and GSM.
- "Returns are a hassle" → exchange-first flow, clear 30-day policy.
- "Arrive in time?" → delivery ETA on the product page, order-by dates.
- "Why pay more than Shein?" → stitching close-ups, fabric weight, brand story.

## Compliance and claims
- FTC Textile and Wool Acts: fiber content by generic name and % by weight, country of origin, company name or RN number. Online listings must show fiber content and "Made in USA" or "Imported".
- Care Labeling Rule (16 CFR 423): permanent care label with at least one safe care method.
- "Bamboo" fabric is usually rayon: say "rayon made from bamboo" (FTC has fined retailers over this).
- "Made in USA" only if all or virtually all US-made. "Organic" needs GOTS/OCS; avoid unqualified "eco"/"sustainable" (Green Guides).
- Disclose imitation leather; "vegan leather" (PU) must never read as genuine leather.
- Kidswear: CPSIA applies (see kids pack).
- Jewelry: lead/cadmium limits (CA and other states, stricter for kids); EU REACH nickel limits. "Hypoallergenic"/"tarnish-free" only if the material backs it (316L steel, 925 silver, plating thickness).
- Sunglasses: FDA impact-resistance (drop-ball) required; "UV400"/"polarized" only with test data.
- Shapewear: no medical claims (fat burning, posture correction, lymphatic drainage).
- CBP detains cotton goods linked to Xinjiang (UFLPA); get supplier traceability.
- IP: no logos, copied prints or "[Brand] dupe" titles/ads (takedowns, ad account bans).
- EU/UK: fiber naming per EU Reg 1007/2011, 14-day withdrawal right, size conversion.

## Seasonality
- Jan: clearance, activewear. Feb: Valentine's (lingerie, date-night).
- Mar–Apr: spring, Easter, festival season (mid-Apr), wedding-guest season begins.
- May–Jun: Mother's Day, graduation, swim/vacation peak (swim sells Mar–Jul).
- Jul–Aug: Prime Day discount pressure; back-to-school (mid-Jul to early Sep), the biggest non-Q4 window for teen/college.
- Sep–Oct: fall layering (knits, jackets, boots), Halloween accessories.
- Nov–Dec: BFCM, party/NYE (sequins, satin), gifting (jewelry, loungewear); ~Dec 15–18 last shipping push.
- Prep: shoot collections 8–10 weeks ahead; reorder winners within 4–6 weeks; stock swim and outerwear 3–4 months ahead; plan around Chinese New Year.

## Creative angles
- Formats: try-on hauls, GRWM, "one piece styled 5 ways", outfit transitions, fabric stretch close-ups.
- Proof: reviews with height/weight, customer photos, sell-out history.
- Angles:
  1. "5'2" and finally found jeans I don't have to hem": fit problem, then solution.
  2. "Same dress on a size 4, 12 and 18": size-inclusive try-on.
  3. "One blazer, five outfits": capsule value.
  4. "Wedding-guest dress under $60 that got me four compliments".
  5. "Feels like pajamas, looks like work": comfort vs polish.

## Price norms and margins
- Bands: tops/tees $20–45; dresses $35–80; denim $50–90; outerwear $70–180; jewelry/accessories $15–50.
- AOV: $55–95, typically 1.8–2.5 units per order.
- Landed COGS: 15–30% (jewelry 5–15%); price at 3–5x landed to absorb returns. US de minimis ended Aug 2025, so duties and tariffs (verify current rates) apply to every parcel.
- Returns: online apparel typically 20–30%, dresses/jeans 30–40%+; ~$10–20 per return to process.
- Compare-at: 20–50%, genuine former price only.
- Bundles: multipacks (tees, socks, underwear), "complete the look", buy 2 save 10–15%, free-shipping threshold $75–100.

## Products and suppliers
- Evergreen: basics, loungewear, denim, activewear, gold-tone stainless jewelry, bags.
- Trend: TikTok micro-trends last 2–8 weeks; test fast in small batches.
- Quality pitfalls: Asian sizing runs 1–2 sizes small (measure samples, publish your own chart), batch-to-batch sizing drift, see-through fabric, color variance, pilling, chemical smell.
- Return drivers: fit (roughly half or more), quality vs photos, color, late delivery.
- Shipping: light polymailers; padded boxes for jewelry.
- Vetting: sample 3 sizes, measure and wash-test, recheck every restock, use POD for prints.

## Red flags
- Counterfeits, logo dupes, copied artwork, stolen model photos.
- "One size fits all" that doesn't; mislabeled fiber ("100% cotton" blends, unqualified "bamboo").
- Medical shapewear, "detox" or magnetic/copper "therapy" jewelry claims.
- Slow shipping on event wear; "no returns" policies (they become chargebacks).
