## performance-data

Ad and store performance data sits in a few SQLite tables (apps/server/src/db.ts). There are no ad set, ad or creative-level metrics. Ad data is campaign-only: `campaigns` (PK network+id, has store_id, name, status, daily_budget) and `campaign_daily` (PK network+campaign_id+date, no store_id). campaign_daily holds five raw metrics per campaign per day: spend, revenue (the network's attributed purchase value), purchases, clicks, impressions. Store-level numbers live in `daily_metrics` (PK store_id+date): revenue, ad_spend, cogs, orders, sessions, add_to_carts, checkouts. `channel_daily` holds Amazon-as-a-channel totals, and `orders`/`order_items` hold per-order and per-product sales for real stores. All three connectors (meta.ts, google.ts, tiktok.ts) ask for campaign-level daily reports and upsert the same five columns. Meta uses level=campaign with time_increment=1, Google uses GAQL FROM campaign with segments.date, TikTok uses data_level AUCTION_CAMPAIGN. They backfill 730 days, then re-pull a 28/30-day attribution window. Each sync ends with rebuildAdSpend, which rewrites daily_metrics.ad_spend as the sum of campaign spend. Derived metrics are worked out at read time and scattered: ROAS in analytics.campaignStats, list_campaigns and reviewCampaigns; CPA in reviewCampaigns, list_campaigns and the Analytics.tsx table; CTR in list_campaigns (ctr_pct) and Analytics.tsx:447. CPC, CPM and per-campaign CVR are not computed anywhere. Store CVR is only implied by summarize().funnel and checkThemeOutcomes. Creatives (`creatives` table) are a library only. They have kind (image/video/ugc/copy), aspect, prompt, headline, primary_text, cta, media_url, source and run_id. There are no hook, angle, length or format-taxonomy fields, and nothing links a creative to a campaign, so no performance can be attributed to one. The demo seeds 4 identical-mix campaigns per store (pawsome, glow, peak) with 730 days of daily_metrics and campaign_daily, 6 products, about 7 days of orders without order_items, 4 creatives, and 1 pending approval per store. Learn-from-outcome logic already exists in apps/server/src/routes/agents-outcomes.ts. Five checkers (ads, products, prices, pages, themes) each judge approved changes once, write a store_memory 'learning' tagged ['outcome', <area>], and nudge the confidence of that run's learnings with judgeRun (±0.1). promotionCandidates/proposeNichePromotions turn learnings that repeat across stores into niche_learnings once the owner approves.

### Gaps
- No ad set, ad or creative grain anywhere: all three connectors request campaign-level reports only (meta.ts:102, google.ts:113, tiktok.ts:81). Comparing hooks or creatives needs new tables (adsets/ads/ad_daily) and new connector queries (Meta level=ad, GAQL FROM ad_group_ad, TikTok AUCTION_AD).
- Nothing links a creative to a campaign or ad: the creatives table (db.ts:285) has no campaign_id/ad_id/network ref. The library can't be joined to performance.
- Creatives have no descriptive attributes beyond kind, aspect, headline, primary_text, cta and prompt. There is no hook type, angle, format taxonomy, video length (Higgsfield video duration is hardcoded 5s in the request, agents-creatives.ts:36, and not stored), product_id or offer.
- Metrics stored per campaign-day are only spend, revenue, purchases, clicks and impressions. There are no add-to-cart/landing-page/video-view/thumb-stop/frequency/reach metrics, and CPC, CPM and campaign CVR are not computed anywhere (CTR only in tools.ts:403 and Analytics.tsx:447, CPA in reviewCampaigns and list_campaigns).
- No campaign metadata for an analyzer: no objective, audience/targeting, placement, start date or bid strategy. campaigns only has name, status and daily_budget (lifetime budgets sync as 0).
- Demo orders have no order_items (demo.ts:280, 237), so product and price outcome checkers skip demo stores (s.demo = 0 filters). Demo per-product numbers are synthesized by demoTopProducts.
- No per-product ad spend, which checkProductOutcomes notes at agents-outcomes.ts:187, so product-level ROAS is impossible.
- Outcome learnings are plain store_memory text rows (kind 'learning', confidence 0.5/0.6/0.7, tags ['outcome', area]) with evidence as a free-text string. There is no structured evidence (metric, window, baseline, delta, sample size) a KB card or confidence ladder could reference.
- No statistical significance anywhere: thresholds are MIN_UNITS=10 and MIN_ORDERS=30 (agents-outcomes.ts:264-266), and ad outcomes use 7d vs 3d per-day averages.

### Risks
- Store scoping of ad data always goes through campaigns.store_id: campaign_daily has no store_id, so every query must JOIN campaigns c ON c.network = d.network AND c.id = d.campaign_id. The campaigns PK is (network, id), global and not per store, and claimCampaigns (common.ts:123) can move a campaign and its whole history to another store.
- daily_metrics.ad_spend is derived: rebuildAdSpend (common.ts:142) overwrites it from campaign_daily on each sync. Don't write ad_spend directly, and remember a new ad-level table would double-count if it is also summed.
- Campaign 'revenue' is the network's attributed purchase value (Meta omni_purchase value, Google conversions_value, TikTok complete_payment_roas*spend), not store revenue, and it is not reconciled with daily_metrics.revenue. In the demo the campaign mix's weighted relative ROAS is about 1.13, so campaign revenue runs roughly 13% above store revenue on average.
- Demo ad data is noise relative to agent actions: demoDay generates campaign_daily for all 4 campaigns every day regardless of campaigns.status or daily_budget (demo.ts:68-73, 110). A paused demo campaign keeps spending, and approved demo changes (agents-actions.ts:131-132) only edit the campaigns row. checkAdOutcomes still judges demo stores (synced() passes with no integrations, and sinceLive passes for demo), so a KB that learns from demo outcomes learns from random numbers.
- All three demo stores share the same 4 campaign names and the same network mix (demo.ts:49-54), even peak (a tiktok_shop store). Cross-store promotion (promotionCandidates) over demo data would find false 'repeats in 2+ stores'. proposeNichePromotions guards this by requiring stores to be live and activated.
- Demo history grows: topUp (demo.ts:209-214) extends from the last stored day and never prunes. It starts with 730 daily_metrics and 2920 campaign_daily rows per demo store (today-729..today; seeded today that is 2024-10-28..2026-10-27), and row counts rise with every day the app runs.
- switchToReal (common.ts:46) wipes demo daily_metrics, products, orders, campaigns and events (campaign_daily cascades) on the first real sync, and startFresh (agents.ts:380) deletes squad learnings, niche_learnings and non-Higgsfield creatives. KB cards derived from demo data need the same go-live purge, or they will leak into a live store.
- Re-sync windows rewrite history: Meta/TikTok re-pull 28 days and Google 30 days of campaign_daily each sync, a full re-sync (cursor NULL, e.g. after a currency change at common.ts:64) re-pulls 730 days, and pruneCampaigns deletes campaigns the account no longer lists. Facts snapshotted from these numbers can go stale, so store the window and the as-of date.
- Amounts are in the store's currency, converted at sync time (adRate/backfillRate), and analytics.ts converts again to a base currency for multi-store views. Stores without an FX rate are silently dropped from campaignStats and loadRows. Cross-store learnings must use converted values or ratios.
- Day boundaries are per zone: ad accounts date days in their own zone (adZone/AD_ZONES), stores in theirs, and dayShifts/ownDays align them. Naive date joins between campaign_daily and daily_metrics can be off by a day for stores in a different zone.
- Outcome judgments are one-shot via done_once refs (e.g. 'ads-outcome:<approvalId>'). Re-running logic under the same ref silently does nothing, and deleted outcomes stay deleted, so a new KB pipeline needs its own ref namespace.
- listCreatives with no storeIds returns every store's creatives, and DELETE /api/creatives/:id is not store-scoped (agents-creatives.ts:63, 73). Store isolation for a per-store KB must be enforced at the call site, as tools.ts:590 does.

## web-fetch-research

The app has no general-purpose safe URL fetcher and no robots.txt handling. Server-side, the only hardened outbound path is the competitor price watch in apps/server/src/competitors.ts. There, `publicFetch` (L108-113) DNS-resolves each request's host and refuses any answer that falls in a node:net BlockList of loopback, private, link-local, CGNAT and IPv6 ULA/link-local ranges (L99-101). The module-private `get` (L128-136) follows redirects itself (`redirect: 'manual'`, at most 5 hops, each hop checked again by isPrivateHost on the name and by publicFetch on DNS), with a 20 s `AbortSignal.timeout` and `accept: application/json`. That path has no response size cap (bodies go straight into `res.json()`), no content-type check, no User-Agent, no per-host rate limit and no caching, and its own comment (L106) admits a DNS-rebinding gap. Everything else calls plain `fetch` against fixed vendor APIs: platform connectors, ECB/currency-api FX, CJ, Higgsfield. Of those, only the sync loop wraps calls in `retrying` (connectors/common.ts:341-351: 429 waits for Retry-After up to 60 s, 5xx or a dropped connection backs off 2/4/8/16 s, 5 tries, no timeout, no User-Agent). CJ has its own module-level 1-call-per-second throttle (cj.ts:21-32). No HTML, readability, PDF, cheerio, turndown or firecrawl code exists: parsing is hand-rolled regex (`decodeHtml` competitors.ts:137, `htmlToText` etsy.ts:189-205, `parseEcb` fx.ts:17-23). jsdom is only a web devDependency for tests, and undici is only pulled in by other packages (electron/get, jsdom, node-gyp). Agents get web information only through the Claude Code built-ins WebSearch and WebFetch, listed per role in packages/agents/src/roles.ts. claude-engine.ts:66 passes them as `tools` and `canUseTool` (L103) allows them with no domain allowlist and no private-IP check, so the app never sees or keeps what they fetch. Product Research (`research_products`, a global table with no store_id) is a 35-row sample catalog seeded at boot (research.ts:147-192). Its only live rows are `find-*` rows that agents write with `record_product_find`, using agent-estimated trend and competition numbers and no source URL or evidence. research.ts itself fetches nothing except FX. Competitor Intel is live for prices only: it reads Shopify `/products.json` + `/meta.json`, the WooCommerce Store API, or the Etsy Open API (only when an Etsy store is connected), once a day per competitor. Competitor ads are demo data only, on `.example` domains; no ad library is read.

### Gaps
- No general safe URL fetcher for arbitrary pages or documents. Missing: response size cap or streaming byte limit (bodies are read whole through res.json(), res.text() or res.arrayBuffer()), content-type allowlist, User-Agent header, per-host rate limit, caching or ETag, and DNS pinning against rebinding.
- No robots.txt fetching or parsing anywhere in apps/ or packages/.
- No HTML main-content extraction (readability, cheerio, turndown, jsdom at runtime): only the regex htmlToText and decodeHtml helpers.
- No PDF or document parsing of any kind, and no upload route for documents.
- No server-side web search: WebSearch exists only inside agent runs as a Claude Code built-in, so a backend research pipeline can't search without starting an agent run.
- The app never sees or stores what WebFetch/WebSearch retrieved. There is no source or citation storage (url, title, fetched_at, content hash, excerpt), so findings can't be cited or deduped against sources.
- research_products has no store_id, source_url, evidence, confidence, fetched_at or relationships columns: a per-store isolated KB and evidence-backed cards need new tables through a new MIGRATIONS entry.
- No research task, queue or job table with status, retry, cancel or per-task budget. Agent runs have maxTurns, maxBudgetUsd and an AbortSignal, but there is no research pipeline state machine.
- No ad library ingestion: competitor ad snapshots exist only as demo seeds or manual POSTs to /api/competitors/:id/snapshots.
- No app-side control over agent web access: no domain allowlist or denylist, no logging of fetched URLs, and canUseTool allows WebFetch and WebSearch unconditionally (claude-engine.ts:103).
- Dedupe of finds is only lower(title) + niche + niche_detail (routes/agents.ts:214-216). There is no fuzzy or semantic dedupe.
- Trend and competition numbers on real finds are agent estimates with no stored basis. There is no learning loop from store or ad performance into research rows.

### Risks
- publicFetch checks DNS and then calls fetch, which resolves the name again (TOCTOU, acknowledged at competitors.ts:106). A DNS-rebinding host can still reach localhost, including the app's own API on 127.0.0.1:8787. A URL-ingest feature exposed to agent-chosen URLs magnifies this.
- isPrivateHost (shared/src/index.ts:1105) is name-only and misses 100.64/10 CGNAT (Tailscale); only publicFetch's BlockList covers that range. Don't rely on isPrivateHost alone for any new fetch path.
- There are no response size limits: a hostile competitor or ingest URL can stream an unbounded body into memory (competitors.ts:145, 167; agents-creatives.ts:53). The competitor watch runs serially in the server process, so a slow or huge site blocks it (20 s timeout per request, up to 10 pages).
- Node fetch sends its default User-Agent and no robots handling exists. Adding broad crawling without both risks blocks and ToS problems. The current watch reads only public JSON catalog endpoints.
- Agent WebFetch bypasses every app guard (claude-engine.ts:103 allows builtins). If 'research from a URL' is built on agent WebFetch, the private-IP rules don't apply. If it's built on server fetch, it must go through publicFetch or a stricter version.
- get() at competitors.ts:130 hardcodes accept: application/json. Reusing it for HTML or PDF needs that header made a parameter, and it's currently not exported.
- retrying() has no timeout and can sleep up to 60 s on a 429. It is documented as for read-only syncs, so don't wrap POSTs or non-idempotent calls in it.
- CJ's throttle state (nextCallAt, cj.ts:21) is module-global: any new CJ caller shares the 1 call per second budget with syncs and searches.
- research.ts:33 marks any row whose id doesn't start with 'find-' as sample, and research_feed hides sample rows for live stores. New KB-derived product rows must keep the 'find-' prefix or a store flag, or they'll be shown or hidden incorrectly.
- Demo and live honesty is enforced in several places (research_feed sample:0, search_suppliers demo refusal, the add-to-store reason, switchToReal deleting demo competitors). A KB that 'learns' from data must skip demo stores and sample rows, or it will turn made-up numbers into Validated knowledge.
- Migrations are an append-only array applied by index (db.ts:9, 528-553). Add new entries; never edit or reorder old ones.
- Any fetched or scraped text returned to an agent must go through csv() or equivalent line-break stripping (tools.ts:92-97). This is regression-tested at agents-competitors.test.ts:84 to stop scraped text from impersonating owner notes or section headers.
- Agent-supplied image URLs are stored unfetched and rendered directly with <img src> in the web UI (Research.tsx:186). Any KB source thumbnails would make the viewer's browser contact third-party hosts.
- apps/desktop/release/win-unpacked/resources/server/server.mjs is a stale bundled copy that shows up in repo-wide greps (for example WebFetch hits). Exclude apps/desktop/release from searches and never edit it.
- track_competitor is capped at 10 per store (routes/agents.ts:191), and every tracked site is read immediately inside the agent's tool call. A research pipeline shouldn't piggyback on that synchronous read inside a turn.

## data-platform

The server is a single-owner local app with no users or roles. Hono serves on 127.0.0.1 only (app.ts:186-193, API_PORT default 8787, index.ts:38). The only access control is one middleware at app.ts:55-63: it returns 403 for a non-local Host (DNS rebinding) or an Origin with a different host:port, and it sets frame-ancestors 'none' and X-Frame-Options DENY. Errors come out as JSON `{ error }` through onError (app.ts:49-52), which also writes to the log, and notFound (app.ts:53). createApp(db, secretKey?) (app.ts:45) builds one chained Hono app. Core endpoints are inline, and feature modules mount with `.route('/', xRoutes(db, pickStores?))` (app.ts:132-179). agentRoutes (lane `agents`, routes/agents.ts:485) owns the current Knowledge Base endpoints: /api/knowledge, /api/stores/:id/memory, /api/memory/:id and /api/niche-learnings (agents.ts:602-645). The schema is one append-only MIGRATIONS array in db.ts:9-525, currently 43 entries. Each entry is a SQL string run inside tx(), with the version tracked in PRAGMA user_version (db.ts:553-558). Tables are created with plain CREATE TABLE, not IF NOT EXISTS, and schema changes use ALTER TABLE ADD COLUMN or RENAME. openDb copies the file DB into backups/ before running pending migrations (db.ts:544-552). It also applies a staged restore (db.ts:531-538, 562-569). Store isolation works by convention, not by a helper layer. `?stores=a,b` goes through pickStores (app.ts:30-34), queries use an inline `store_id IN (?,?,..)` guarded by `if (!storeIds.length) return []`, and FKs use `REFERENCES stores(id) ON DELETE CASCADE`. PATCH and DELETE by id (/api/memory/:id, /api/competitors/:id) do not check which store a row belongs to. Validation is hand-written per module. There is no zod in the server; zod lives only in packages/agents/src/tools.ts:3 for agent tool schemas. The pattern is parseX(body, partial) returning either an error string or the clean fields, and the route turns a string into 400 `{ error }`. Types and API contracts live in packages/shared/src/index.ts (one 1297-line file), with endpoint docs written as JSDoc above each interface. That file is additive-only (header lines 1-2, CLAUDE.md). Backups use node:sqlite backup() on the whole database (backups.ts:37-57), so every table and FTS index is included automatically. Files in the data dir are not included: Knowledge Base markdown edits in knowledge/, custom niche packs in packs/niche/, and creatives media. Tests are vitest from the root config: 149 files and 992 tests per the latest backlog entry. The config forces FRONTLINE_AGENT_ENGINE=fake and a temp FRONTLINE_DATA_DIR. Route tests build `openDb()` (in-memory), `seedDemo(db)` and `createApp(db)`, then call `app.request(path, init)` with no server.

### Risks
- Route shadowing: agentRoutes registers `/api/knowledge/:kind/:key` for GET, PUT and DELETE (routes/agents.ts:624-638) and is mounted at app.ts:178. Any new `/api/knowledge/<a>/<b>` route mounted after it is answered by that handler first (404 'Not found' or a markdown save). Likewise researchRoutes (mounted at app.ts:174) has GET `/api/research/:id` (research.ts:109), so a later GET `/api/research/tasks` would be read as a product id. Fix by mounting new KB and research routes earlier, or by using fresh prefixes (e.g. /api/kb/..., /api/research-tasks/...).
- Migrations: append-only and never edited. Each runs inside BEGIN/COMMIT (db.ts:553-558), so `PRAGMA foreign_keys = OFF` table rebuilds do not work inside a migration (the pragma is a no-op inside a transaction). Prefer ADD COLUMN with a DEFAULT, or new tables. CREATE TABLE is not IF NOT EXISTS, so a name clash fails the whole startup.
- An update with new migrations VACUUMs a full copy into backups/ on first start (db.ts:544-552). Large KB tables (documents, transcripts) make every update and the daily backup slower and bigger, and backups keep 7 copies (backups.ts:11).
- Restore refuses a snapshot whose user_version is greater than MIGRATIONS.length (backups.ts:110), and openDb denies pending real-store approvals after a restore (db.ts:562-569). Approval-gated KB actions are affected by this too.
- Lane ownership: db.ts, app.ts, shared and the non-agents server modules belong to the core lane. routes/agents*.ts and packages/agents/** (including the current /api/knowledge endpoints, memory.ts and prompt.ts) belong to the agents lane. With one worker this is a single owner, but CLAUDE.md still asks for lane-labelled commits and backlog entries.
- packages/shared is additive-only (index.ts:1-2). Do not rename or remove KnowledgeKind, KnowledgeDoc, StoreMemory, MemoryInput or NicheLearning; extend with new optional fields or types. The web app and the agents read them.
- Test isolation: vitest.config.ts forces a temp FRONTLINE_DATA_DIR and the fake engine, and db.test.ts:79 asserts the data dir is under tmpdir. A file-writing test that needs a clean dir uses vi.stubEnv plus vi.unstubAllEnvs, never `delete`. File-I/O tests have needed 15 s timeouts under the full 992-test suite (agents-knowledge.test.ts:36, 56). Never use the real engine in tests.
- The dev server's data folder is apps/server/.data (pnpm dev runs from apps/server; docs/backlog/core.md:300). The desktop app's is <userData>/data (apps/desktop/src/main.ts:174). Do not hardcode a path; always go through dataDir().
- SSRF: KB URL ingestion must reuse publicFetch plus manual redirects with isPrivateHost (competitors.ts:108-136). Its own ponytail note says a DNS-rebinding host could still get past it.
- Exit code 75 (RESTART_CODE) restarts the API under the desktop app only; under pnpm dev the restore route just exits (backups.ts:132). Long-running research jobs must survive a restart: agent runs are recovered by state in the DB, not in memory.
- The local-only middleware (app.ts:55-63) refuses requests from other origins, including other local ports. A separate canvas dev tool on another port cannot call the API directly.
- node:sqlite is synchronous. A heavy FTS or relationship query, or a big write, blocks the whole API, which has happened before (migrations 25-26 added indexes after 4-8 s stalls). Index store_id plus sort columns and keep JS-side scoring bounded (memory.ts:128 ponytail note: fine to a few thousand rows per store).

## knowledge-memory

I mapped the knowledge and memory layer as it stands. Nothing was edited, and the working tree is clean. agents-memory.test.ts passes, 13 of 13. The layer has three tiers. (1) Static expertise layers are markdown files on disk: 8 role playbooks (packages/agents/playbooks/<role>.md), 6 platform packs and 8 niche packs, plus one _template.md (packages/agents/packs/{platform,niche}/), about 129 KB in total. Each is an H1 title over H2 sections. prompt.ts `layer()` reads them, using the owner's override at <FRONTLINE_DATA_DIR>/knowledge/<path> first when one exists. A custom niche ('other') gets an approval-gated pack, drafted by the Market Analyst, saved at <FRONTLINE_DATA_DIR>/packs/niche/<slug>.md. (2) Per-store memory lives in SQLite: the `store_memory` table plus an external-content FTS5 index `store_memory_fts(text, tags)`, porter unicode61, kept in sync by triggers (migrations 4, 19 and 20 in db.ts). It holds kinds fact, learning and preference, with free-text evidence, a 0..1 confidence and a JSON tags array. Writes merge on exact lowercased text per store. There is a cap of 300 agent facts and learnings per store, and pins, preferences and owner notes never count against it. Two app-only tags exist: `outcome` (measured results) and `denied` (the owner's no). (3) `niche_learnings` (migration 14) holds owner-approved learnings shared by every store in a niche, keyed by nicheKey (a niche id, or other:<slug>). Knowledge reaches agents in two ways. First, runs.ts:241 builds the system prompt with assemblePrompt. It gets the 12 best memories from searchMemory(db, storeId, run.task, 12), and the pins, owner notes and denials always come on top of those 12. It also gets listNicheLearnings(nicheKey, 20). The prompt order is fixed: role, playbook, platform pack, niche or custom pack, niche learnings, store profile, then memories, scheduled jobs, waiting requests and the date. The whole prompt has an 8000-token budget (chars/4), and memories always get at least 1500 tokens. Second, the in-process MCP server "frontline" exposes the tools search_memory, write_memory and promote_niche_learning. Learning happens in four ways. Any run that made tool calls gets a same-session REFLECTION_PROMPT afterwards, parsed by parseLearnings and saved with writeMemory using the run's runId. Approval denials become `denied` preference memories (rememberDenial), but closedByApp '[Expired: …]' requests are skipped. Outcome checks in agents-outcomes.ts write `outcome` learnings and call judgeRun, which moves every memory of that run by ±0.1, once per ref via the done_once ledger. An hourly proposeNichePromotions turns learnings found on their own in 2+ live stores of a niche (Jaccard ≥0.5, confidence ≥0.7) into niche.promote_learning approvals. When a store goes live, startFresh deletes the squad's demo facts and learnings and that store's niche_learnings. The owner side is Hono routes in apps/server/src/routes/agents.ts (lines 602-645) and the UI-16 page apps/web/src/pages/Knowledge.tsx, which has 4 tabs: playbook, platform, niche and memory. The layer has no general knowledge base in the database, no card types, sources, citations, relationships, research jobs or embeddings.

### Gaps
- No General (cross-store, non-niche) knowledge store in the DB. General expertise exists only as shipped markdown files, and store_memory.store_id is NOT NULL with ON DELETE CASCADE.
- No card types beyond MemoryKind fact|learning|preference, and no confidence ladder (Hypothesis -> Research-Supported -> Experimentally Tested -> Validated). Confidence is a bare 0..1 float with no state or transition history.
- No structured sources or citations. 'evidence' is one free-text column of at most 300 chars, there is no sources table (url, title, fetched_at, hash), and memories carry no source ids.
- No typed relationships or edges between memories or cards, so no graph and no relationship-aware retrieval.
- No research pipeline: no research_tasks/jobs table, plan/discovery/retrieval/extraction stages, per-task budget, retry or cancel beyond agent runs, and no report artifact. 'research_feed' is core's product-research feed, a different thing.
- No 'research from a URL or document' ingestion. Agents can WebFetch, but nothing stores fetched documents or chunks, and there is no upload path for documents.
- Dedupe is exact lowercased-text only per store (writeMemory L31). There is no fuzzy or semantic dedupe at write time, and Jaccard exists only in promotionCandidates.
- Markdown layers are not searchable or retrievable by chunk. Each is injected whole for the agent's role, platform and niche, and agents have no tool to search playbooks or packs.
- Retrieval tool search_memory returns no author or provenance label and no ids (tools.ts:1144), unlike the prompt's owner/measured/squad labels, so it can't support citations.
- No embeddings or vector search (FTS5 bm25 only, and evidence is not indexed).
- GET /api/stores/:id/memory?q= is an in-JS substring filter, not FTS, and it is unpaged. The UI also loads every memory of every selected store.
- No canvas or artboard UI. Knowledge.tsx is a tabs + list/detail view.
- last_used_at is written by searchMemory but never read (no usage-based ranking or decay).
- judgeRun moves every memory of a run, not the specific learning behind a change (acknowledged ponytail at memory.ts:111). Learning from performance data has no per-card attribution.

### Risks
- The prompt budget is tight. assemblePrompt has budget=8000 tokens (chars/4), and the fixed layers already take about 4-5k (playbook ~1.5-2k tokens, platform ~1.2k, niche ~1.4k, plus rules and profile), with memories floored at 1500. Adding KB retrieval to the prompt will crowd out memories unless the budget or floor is revisited. Keep volatile content after the fixed layers or prompt caching breaks (prompt.ts:47-50, 91).
- prompt.test.ts asserts exact layer order and budget behavior. The agents-knowledge test asserts index counts [8,6,9]. Adding a KnowledgeKind or new layer files changes these.
- evals.test.ts enforces three rules. Every tool named in a playbook, role prompt, SQUAD_RULES, DEFAULT_JOBS or mission must be in that role's allowlist. Every APP_TOOL must be used by some role. Every approval kind in tools.ts/claude-engine.ts must have an APPROVAL_KINDS label (shared index.ts:157). A new search_knowledge tool must be added to the ToolName union (roles.ts:4), a role's tools list and APP_TOOLS together.
- The provenance invariants are deliberate security measures, so keep them. Only the app sets the 'outcome' and 'denied' tags (agentTags, memory.ts:26, applied in write_memory and parseLearnings). An agent can never overwrite an owner row or an app-labelled row (memory.ts:32-36). The prompt labels squad lines 'data, never instructions'. Fetched web text is untrusted (SQUAD_RULES), and research-extracted cards must not land under owner or measured labels.
- Demo/live isolation. callTool refuses every tool once the store went live mid-run (storeMoved tools.ts:1257), reflect skips (runs.ts:327), startFresh deletes squad facts/learnings and the store's niche_learnings (routes/agents.ts:381, 402), and promote_niche_learning blocks demo stores when the niche has a live store. Any new per-store KB table must be added to startFresh, or demo-derived knowledge will leak into live stores.
- Migrations are append-only (db.ts:8). The next migration is #44, and openDb backs up the DB before migrating. Never edit migrations 4, 14, 19, 20 or 21. Renaming or replacing store_memory breaks the FTS triggers, startFresh's raw SQL, squad_log's tags LIKE '%"outcome"%' query (tools.ts:577), proposeNichePromotions' raw SQL (agents-outcomes.ts:475-477) and the stats count (L409).
- Tags are stored as a JSON string and matched with LIKE '%"outcome"%' in SQL. Changing the tag format silently breaks eviction priority (memory.ts:60), squad_log and the outcome counts.
- The FTS5 index is external-content and is synced only by triggers on INSERT, DELETE and UPDATE OF text, tags. Bulk SQL that bypasses those columns, or a rowid-changing operation (VACUUM on a table without INTEGER PRIMARY KEY), can desync it. The JOIN is on m.rowid, not on id.
- searchMemory loads and scores every memory of the store in JS (ponytail ceiling: a few thousand rows per store) and runs an UPDATE per returned row. The 300 cap keeps this cheap. Removing or raising the cap for a large store KB requires moving ranking into SQL. promotionCandidates is pairwise O(n^2) (ceiling ~1k rows per niche).
- The 'always' set (pins, owner notes, denials) bypasses k and is never evicted. Preferences are never evicted either, so agent-inferred preferences and denials grow without bound (accepted in AGT-121). A KB that turns more things into 'always' rows will bloat every prompt.
- knowledge.ts all() reads every markdown file from disk on each GET /api/knowledge and knowledgeDoc call (no cache), and the layer() read runs per run start. That is fine for 23 files and not for hundreds.
- Knowledge edits and custom packs live under FRONTLINE_DATA_DIR (./.data default). Tests rely on vitest.config.ts setting a temp FRONTLINE_DATA_DIR, and the knowledge, niche and activate tests use vi.stubEnv. New file-backed KB tests must do the same or they touch real owner data.
- There are two once() implementations on the same done_once table (packages/agents/src/memory.ts:104 and apps/server/src/db.ts:577). Ref prefixes are a shared namespace (ads:, product:, price:, page:, theme:, outcome-note:, promote-memory:, activate:, retried:, not-applied:), so new research jobs must pick unique prefixes.
- Reflection runs only when a run made tool calls and has budget left, and the fake engine always returns '[]'. Tests of learning flows must seed memory directly or script the fake engine.

## agent-engine-jobs

Everything an agent does is one row in `agent_runs` (db.ts:207, migration 7, plus `parent_run_id` from migration 12). There is no separate job table: the queue is that table with status='queued'. `createRunManager` (packages/agents/src/runs.ts:32) pumps queued rows in created_at order. Limits: 3 runs at once overall, 2 per store, and never two runs of the same agent at once (runs.ts:157-177). For each run it builds a RunInput with assemblePrompt, the top 12 memories from searchMemory, niche learnings, open approvals and scheduled jobs, plus the run's AbortSignal and budget share. It then drives `runAgent` (engine.ts:47), which guarantees the stream ends with exactly one done or error event. Every event goes to the in-memory transcript and to `onEvent`. The app singleton `runManager(db)` (routes/agents.ts:303) wires onEvent/onRun to `publish()` on the in-process live bus (live.ts:120). The bus feeds the `/api/live` WebSocket (app.ts:91-99) as `run_event` and `run` messages. In the browser, `useLive` (apps/web/src/api/client.ts:523) folds those into the react-query `['run', id]` cache via `applyRunEvent` (apps/web/src/lib/transcript.ts:31).

The Claude engine (claude-engine.ts:62) calls the SDK `query()` with:
- settingSources [], strictMcpConfig and skills [];
- `tools` = the role's capitalized built-ins only (WebSearch/WebFetch);
- an in-process MCP server named `frontline` with the role's app tools;
- a `canUseTool` gate, maxTurns 40, maxBudgetUsd = the run's share, and resume.

Demo (unconnected) stores and all vitest runs use the fake engine, which plays scripted steps and runs tools for real. After any successful run that called tools, a same-session reflection query (REFLECTION_PROMPT) turns learnings into store_memory rows and `memory_written` events (runs.ts:325-344). Budgets are enforced per store per day (`StoreCaps.agentCostPerDay`, default $5) and optionally per agent (`dailyBudgetUsd`). Spend is persisted live through `onSpend`, and a run aborts as soon as `costToday` passes either cap (runs.ts:209-226).

Agents delegate with the `assign_task` tool (tools.ts:925): it starts a child run (`parent_run_id`), at most 3 per run, and a run that was itself assigned cannot pass work on. Squad missions (missions.ts) are a DAG plan stored in `squad_missions.steps_json` over ordinary runs. Each step's status is read off its run, and `advanceMissions` starts the next step when a run ends, on startup and every 60 s. Approvals are rows in `approvals`. Tools call `requestApproval`, which inserts and broadcasts the request, emits `approval_request`, and awaits `approvals.decision()` — a promise resolved off the live bus. A run waiting on the owner gives up its slot. Kinds in OUTLIVE_RUN are carried out server-side by `onApproved` handlers even after the run is gone. Scheduling is `automation_rules` (trigger: cron or metric; action is always `{type:'agent_task', agentId, task}`), checked every 60 s by `startScheduler` (automations.ts:211), with catch-up for missed cron slots up to 31 days back. The 8 default jobs are seeded per store from jobs.ts DEFAULT_JOBS.

How the research task should use this:
- **Machinery to reuse.** Model the research task as agent runs. One run per pipeline phase, chained as a squad mission (plan in steps_json, each step a run with hand-off text), gives per-step checkpoints for free. A single long run would hit the 40-turn cap and hold the agent and a store slot.
- **Progress to the UI.** Use the existing `run_event` / `run` / `mission` live messages. KB tools can push their own events with `ctx.emit`, the same way write_memory emits `memory_written` (tools.ts:1159).
- **Cancel.** `POST /api/runs/:id/cancel` calls runs.cancel: a queued run is marked cancelled right away; a running one is aborted, which ends it as cancelled. `POST /api/squad-missions/:id/cancel` calls cancelMission. Requests the run made that aren't in OUTLIVE_RUN are then closed as denied.
- **Recovery after a restart.** At startup every queued or running row is marked error 'Interrupted by an app restart', and its non-OUTLIVE approvals are closed as denied. A run created in the last 6 hours (not a chat follow-up) is retried once, from scratch, as a new run linked by `retry:<old>:<new>` in done_once. Missions follow that link, and `advanceSquadMissions` runs at startup (agents.ts:490), so a mission resumes from its last finished step. Work inside a single run is not resumed.

### Gaps
- No store-less runs: agent_runs.store_id is NOT NULL with an FK to stores (db.ts:207-209), agents exist only per store (`${storeId}:${role}`), and budgets, caps, slots and automations are all per store. A General knowledge base research task has no agent, store or budget to run under without a new home (a system store/agent, or a store-less run path).
- No research role: the 8 roles are fixed (AgentRole in shared/index.ts:43, ROLES, AGENT_ROLES, SQUAD_RULES 'one of eight', createSquad, UI portraits, evals). Either give KB tools to market_analyst/product_hunter, which already have WebSearch+WebFetch, or add a 9th role across all of these.
- No step or progress model inside one run: progress is only the event transcript. There is no per-phase status, percent or checkpoint. Missions give step-level state but each step is a full run with text hand-off only (no structured payload between steps).
- No generic retry: the only retry is the one-time post-restart rerun within 6 hours (runs.ts:348-351). start() has a retryOf parameter but no API exposes it. There is no backoff, no attempt count and no 'retry failed run' route.
- No per-task budget: only store/day (StoreCaps.agentCostPerDay) and agent/day (dailyBudgetUsd). A per-research-task USD cap needs a new parameter through start() -> pump/execute -> RunInput.budgetUsd.
- maxTurns is fixed at 40 for every run (claude-engine.ts:121), and the STOPPED message names it. A longer research run needs a per-input override through ClaudeEngineOptions.queryOptions (spread last at l.124), keyed on something RunInput doesn't carry today (e.g. a task kind).
- No knowledge-card storage, tools or citations: only store_memory (one sentence of 5-300 chars, kind fact|learning|preference, evidence up to 300 chars), niche_learnings and markdown knowledge docs. There are no sources, evidence, relationship or confidence-ladder tables, and no KB tools in APP_TOOLS.
- No document or file ingestion path: agents can only WebFetch URLs. There is no upload route, no stored-document table and no extractor tool.
- No new AgentEvent or LiveMessage kinds for research progress: the AgentEvent union is closed in shared. A new event needs shared types, web toBlocks rendering (unknown types are silently skipped), and query invalidation in useLive (client.ts:541-555).
- Transcripts are not a durable progress log: they are written mid-run only on onSpend (Claude engine, per assistant message) or while an approval is pending, plus at the end. The fake engine never calls onSpend, so tests see no mid-run persistence. The live bus has no replay, so a client reconnecting refetches everything.
- Automation actions only support {type:'agent_task'} with a store agent (automations.ts parseRule); there is no 'research_task' action type and no store-less schedule.
- Learning from ad/store performance exists only as judgeRun confidence nudges on store_memory (memory.ts:112) and outcome loops; nothing writes structured performance learnings into a KB.

### Risks
- One agent never runs twice at once, and slots are 3 global / 2 per store (runs.ts:33, 161). A long research run on market_analyst blocks that store's Daily briefing, Weekly price check, Weekly report and Ask AI (Ctrl+K lands on market_analyst) until it ends. Prefer short phase runs (a mission) or a dedicated agent.
- start() de-duplicates: the same agent with the same task text already queued (no resume/parent/retry) returns the existing run (runs.ts:87-96). Two research tasks with identical text collapse into one; put an id or the URL in the task text.
- Restart recovery reruns from scratch, as a new run with the same task, not a resumed session. It happens only if created < 6 h ago, never for runs with `resume`, and only once (runs.ts:348-351). Older interrupted runs stay error 'Interrupted by an app restart'. Expensive multi-step research should checkpoint each phase in its own rows or mission steps.
- Pending approvals whose kind is not in OUTLIVE_RUN are closed as denied when their run ends without 'done' and at startup (expireApprovals, runs.ts:44-56, 61). A KB 'save these cards' approval would be lost on restart unless its kind is added to OUTLIVE_RUN with an onApproved executor.
- approvals.decision() waits on the in-process bus (approvals.ts:92-110). Nothing waiting survives a restart except automation_runs 'awaiting_approval' rows, which startScheduler re-subscribes (automations.ts:283).
- Every successful run that called tools triggers a reflection query in the same session (runs.ts:263): it costs budget and writes store_memory rows (memory_written). Research runs will spill learnings into store memory unless the reflection is skipped or redirected for them.
- Stored and live tool_result text is clipped to 4000 chars (runs.ts:186, SHOWN at l.356), and runs older than 90 days are trimmed to 200 chars per tool output at startup (runs.ts:363). Never use transcripts as the store of record for fetched sources or evidence.
- callTool refuses every tool once the store went live or changed currency mid-run (tools.ts:1409-1413, storeMoved). On go-live, startFresh deletes the store's agent-written facts/learnings, niche learnings, drafts, demo creatives and running missions (agents.ts:380-411). Per-store KB data made on demo data needs a matching go-live policy, or it will mix demo and real numbers.
- Demo (unconnected) stores always run on the fake engine (engine.ts:34-38), so research there produces canned echo output. The niche-pack route refuses the demo engine for this reason (agents.ts:554); a research route likely should too.
- evals.test.ts will fail unless: every new app tool is in some role's tools, role prompts/playbooks/jobs/mission templates only mention tools that role has, any new SDK built-in is added to the hard-coded SDK_TOOLS, and every new approval kind has an APPROVAL_KINDS label. jobs test: changing a DEFAULT_JOBS task text requires adding the old hash to FORMER_TASKS (jobs.ts:88).
- Budget accounting: costToday counts a run's whole cost on the day it ends (squads.ts:60-71). A run aborts when the store's spend today passes the cap, regardless of its share (runs.ts:218-225). Long research runs can hit the $5/day default and stop mid-pipeline with an 'error' status (not 'cancelled').
- toolEnv.startRun always routes through the runManager(db) singleton (agents.ts:158), even in tests that build their own manager. advanceMissions uses module-level re-entrancy flags (missions.ts:158-159). Mission step runs have parent_run_id set, so assign_task is refused inside them (tools.ts:938-940).
- The live bus is process-wide (live.ts:7) and onDecision handlers filter by DB (agents-actions.ts:23). New listeners must follow the WeakRef/DB-check pattern, or tests with many DBs will cross-fire.
- Adding a migration means appending to MIGRATIONS (the last index is 43, db.ts:522). openDb copies a backup before migrating, and migration order and indices must never change.
