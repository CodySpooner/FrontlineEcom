Validated insight

Problem-first hooks beat product-first hooks

Pawsome Gear › Advertising › Video hooks
HypothesisSupportedTestedValidated

Across two hook tests, videos that opened on a travel problem (car anxiety, a messy back seat, a hot car) beat videos that opened on the product. Same audience, budget split and offer both times.

EVIDENCE

MEASURED EXP-14 · 14 days · $1,820 spend · 2.9 vs 1.6 ROAS · 71 orders
MEASURED Held in a second run, EXP-17 · 2.6 vs 1.5 ROAS
RESEARCH 3 of 4 sources say problem-first openers lead in pet ads

APPLIES TO

TikTok and Meta Reels · cold audiences · dog owners

LIMITS

Untested on Google Shopping and retargeting. Both runs were in fall.

HOW AGENTS USE IT

Creative Studio opens new pet-travel videos on a problem. Ad Strategist tests one new problem a week.
